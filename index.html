JamScan Quick Transfer uses one simple static QR to pair two devices. The actual photos, videos, text, and files move through an encrypted WebRTC connection instead of being squeezed through the camera.

Quick SendCreate a temporary pairing QR and transfer megabytes directly between browsers. Quick ReceiveNormally opened automatically after scanning a sender's pairing QR. Offline OpticalUse animated QR frames only when the devices cannot make a network connection. Open .jscanVerify and preview a saved JamScan package.
