This file is general project information, not legal advice.

## Accessibility

The project includes accessibility work and a testing checklist, but it does not guarantee compliance with any law or standard. Accessibility obligations can depend on the organization, location, audience, service, and deployment.

Before commercial, government, school, health, financial, or other high-impact use, obtain:

- A professional accessibility audit
- Manual assistive-technology testing
- Testing with disabled users
- Legal advice for the applicable jurisdiction

## Flashing and changing patterns

JamScan visual streams use changing high-contrast black-and-white patterns. The project caps selectable playback at 3 frames per second, does not autoplay, shows a warning before playback, supports reduced motion, and offers `.jscan` file transfer as a nonflashing alternative.

No technical control can guarantee that every person will be safe or comfortable. Stop playback immediately if anyone reports discomfort.

## User content

JamScan does not host, moderate, approve, or verify user content. A package may contain misleading labels, scams, inappropriate material, malicious files, or illegal content.

JamScan integrity checks only show whether bytes changed during packaging or transfer. They do not prove that content is safe, truthful, lawful, or malware-free.

## Deployment responsibility

A person or organization deploying JamScan is responsible for:

- Content policies and moderation
- Privacy notices and data handling
- Accessibility testing and fixes
- Security review
- Age-appropriate use
- Local legal requirements
- Hosting and transport security
